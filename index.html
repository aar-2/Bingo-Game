Game Rules & How to Play

Goal of the Game

Complete a winning pattern on your Bingo card before the AI players complete theirs!

How to Play

  1. Select the number of cards you want to play with (1, 2, or 4).
  2. Click CALL NEXT to manually draw numbers, or toggle AUTO-PLAY to have the host draw them automatically at your preferred speed.
  3. Look at your cards. If the called number is on your card, click it to daub (mark) it.
  4. When you complete a target pattern (Line, Double Line, 4 Corners, or Full House), smash the CLAIM BINGO! button to verify and win!

Target Patterns

  • 1 Line: Any full horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line.
  • 2 Lines: Any two completed lines on the same card.
  • 4 Corners: Mark the four corner cells of the card.
  • Full House: Mark every single number on your card!

Special Features

  • 🔊 Synth Sound FX: Custom chimes generated using Web Audio API synthesis.
  • 🎙️ Voice Caller: Synthesized Text-to-Speech voices read out the calls (e.g. "B-12").
  • 🤖 Live AI Competitors: Watch the AI players make progress on their own hidden cards in real-time. Don't let them beat you!
